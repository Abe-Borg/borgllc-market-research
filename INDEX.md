Each run claims one (market, angle) assignment from `ledger.json`, researches it,
commits the report to `reports/`, and marks it complete. Claims are pushed before
research begins so concurrent runs never collide. The backlog grows as runs
discover new markets worth examining.

The four angles are `core-practitioner-workflow`, `narrow-subspecialty`,
`back-office`, and `handoffs-and-qa`.
